    AVAILABLE OFFLINE

    General preparedness reminders. Follow local emergency services and trained responders whenever possible.

    01Prepare an emergency kitEssentials for the first 72 hours+

    Keep drinking water, shelf-stable food, necessary medicines, a flashlight, spare batteries, a power bank, a first-aid kit, and copies of key contacts. Check supplies regularly and adapt them to your household.

    02During floodingMove away from rising water+

    Follow official evacuation instructions. Move to higher ground if it is safe to do so. Never walk or drive through floodwater; depth and current can be deceptive. Avoid downed power lines and report hazards when communication is available.

    03If you discover a fireGet out and raise the alarm+

    Alert others and leave by a safe exit. Stay low if there is smoke, do not use elevators, and do not re-enter. Once safe, contact your local fire service. Only use an extinguisher if the fire is small, you are trained, and you have a clear exit.

    04Basic first-aid prioritiesProtect yourself, then get help+

    Check that the scene is safe before approaching. Contact local emergency services as soon as possible. Follow instructions from trained responders; do not move someone with a suspected serious injury unless they face immediate danger.
